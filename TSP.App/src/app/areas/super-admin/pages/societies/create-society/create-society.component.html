Create New Society

    Super Admin Creates Society

    You're creating the foundation by setting up the society and assigning a faculty advisor to oversee its activities.

  • 2

    Advisor Builds Committee

    The faculty advisor selects and appoints students to form the committee that will lead the society.

  • 3

    Students Request to Join

    Interested students submit membership requests to become part of the society.

  • 4

    Committee Reviews Requests

    The committee reviews and approves membership requests, building the society's membership base.
